Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how this app (the "App") handles user information.

2. Information we collect

This app does not collect any personal information. It never obtains your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or similar data.

3. Storage of learning data

All of your learning progress, word lists, and other data are stored only on your device (in a local iOS database). Theme settings (colors and fonts) are stored in your device's settings storage (UserDefaults). None of this data is ever sent to the developer's servers or to any outside party. If you turn on study reminders (notifications), they are scheduled on your device and are never sent externally.

4. Analytics and tracking

This app uses no analytics tools or ads of any kind and does not track your activity.

5. Links to external sites

This app contains links to external dictionary and search sites (such as RAE, SpanishDict, WordReference, Linguee, and Google Image Search). Tapping a link opens the site in Safari. Information on linked sites is handled according to each site's privacy policy.

6. Sharing with third parties

Because this app does not collect personal information, there is no information to provide to third parties.

7. Billing

This app offers an auto-renewing subscription, the "Pro plan." All purchases, renewals, and cancellations are processed through Apple's App Store, and the developer never obtains or stores your payment information, such as credit card numbers. Your purchase status is also verified on your device using Apple's framework (StoreKit) and is never sent to external servers.

8. Children's privacy

Because this app does not collect personal information, people of all ages can use it with confidence.

9. Changes to this policy

This Policy may be revised as necessary. If there are significant changes, we will notify you through app updates or other means.
